03 · Partner operations

Partner health scoring

Revenue tells you a partner is in trouble about two quarters too late. This score watches the activity that comes before revenue, so I can step in while there's still time.

The idea

Engaged partners build pipeline. Pipeline doesn't build engaged partners. So the score weighs engagement more heavily than revenue, especially in the first year of a partnership.

Six parts, 100 points

PartPointsWhat healthy looks like
Training, workshops and joint planning25Attends enablement, has a current joint plan, executive sponsor joins planning
QBRs and governance25Attends every QBR with an executive, completes action items between reviews
Co-marketing20Active campaigns, uses its funding, shares results
Pipeline activity15New joint opportunities each month, deals moving through stages
Platform adoption10Uses partner tools, demo environments and the partner portal
Team assessment5The partner team's view of relationship quality

Scored by the partner team, not self-reported by partners. Weights shift toward pipeline as a partnership matures.

Four response levels

ScoreLevelResponse
85–100ChampionInvest more: co-marketing, joint pursuits, case studies
70–84ActiveKeep the rhythm, look for the next step up
55–69At riskCall within two weeks, find the cause, agree on a recovery plan
Below 55CriticalExecutive-to-executive conversation and a decision: reset or step back

Finding the cause

The score tells me where to look. The conversation tells me why. The causes I see most often:

  • Something broke on our side, like a product change that disrupted the partner's integration. Fix: escalate to product and close the loop with the partner.
  • The partner reorganized and lost its sponsor. Fix: find and engage a new executive sponsor.
  • People left, so certifications lapsed. Fix: a focused enablement session and a short certification plan.
  • The partnership isn't paying off for them. Fix: revisit the economics, or agree to step back.

How it runs day to day

The score lives in the CRM or partner relationship management system, so nobody rebuilds it in a spreadsheet each quarter.

  • Each part has a field, updated on a regular cycle, with a total score and level calculated automatically.
  • A QBR prep report pulls 90 days of trends, pipeline and open actions before every review.
  • I use AI tools for meeting notes and partner prep, which keeps the data current without extra admin.

Result

I first built this approach at a global enterprise technology company and then applied it in a hyperscaler's strategic partner program. Across that program's seven strategic partners, engagement rose 40% and attrition fell 25%.
