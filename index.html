About

I started my career as a clinical lab scientist working under federal regulatory standards, moved into technical marketing, and found my way to partnerships. That path left me with a habit I still rely on: check the data before you commit, and write down what you agreed to.

Most of my alliance work has been in data and AI. At a global enterprise technology company, I owned partner-led revenue across a 300+ partner portfolio that included seven Tier-1 global systems integrators. I then ran partner operations for a hyperscaler's strategic partner program and managed budget operations for a large hyperscaler marketing organization. Today I lead alliances with four cloud data platforms for a data and AI consultancy.

I use AI every day at work: Claude and Fireflies for partner preparation, meeting notes and follow-ups. On my own time I run an AI-focused YouTube channel, where I build automated workflows in n8n and Make. Building them taught me how to turn a messy process into steps a machine can run reliably.
