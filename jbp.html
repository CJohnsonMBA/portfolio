04 · Governance

Joint business plan and executive QBR

A partnership without a written plan is a friendship. This is the one-page plan and the meeting rhythm I use so both companies know what they signed up for.

The one-page plan

SectionWhat goes in it
Joint valueOne sentence each: what we get, what the partner gets, what the customer gets
FocusTwo or three industries or solution plays, and the target account list
TargetsPartner-sourced pipeline, partner-influenced pipeline, joint wins, certified people
Our commitmentsEnablement, co-sell coverage, funding, technical support
Partner commitmentsCertifications, seller coverage, marketing activity, executive sponsor time
Sponsors and ownersNamed executive sponsor and working lead on each side
RisksWhat could stop us, and who owns each risk

Meeting rhythm

MeetingHow oftenWhoPurpose
Working sessionWeekly or every two weeksAlliance leads, sales and technical leadsAccount-level next steps, blockers
Pipeline reviewMonthlyAlliance leads, sales leadershipJoint pipeline, stuck deals, new pursuits
Executive QBRQuarterlyExecutive sponsors on both sidesResults against plan, decisions, next-quarter priorities
Annual planningYearlyExecutive sponsors and leadsReset targets, commitments and focus

A 45-minute executive QBR

  1. Five minutes: the scoreboard. Targets against actuals, on one page, sent the day before.
  2. Ten minutes: wins and lessons. One or two joint wins, and what we'd repeat.
  3. Fifteen minutes: decisions. The two or three things that need an executive answer: funding, coverage, a stuck deal, a product gap.
  4. Ten minutes: next quarter. Priorities and any change to targets.
  5. Five minutes: commitments. Who does what by when, read back before we close.

Executives should spend their time on decisions, not status updates. If a QBR has no decision on the agenda, it should be an email.

Keeping it honest

  • A decision log, shared with the partner, so nobody relitigates last quarter.
  • A clear escalation path: working lead, then sales leader, then executive sponsor, with a time limit at each step.
  • Agreed definitions of sourced and influenced revenue before the first QBR, so the scoreboard isn't a debate.
